================================================================================
SYSTEM SCOPE, PURPOSE, AND FUNCTIONALITY
Clinic Management System (Patient / Doctor / Admin Portals)
Project: LustreMDC Clinic Management System
================================================================================

I. PURPOSE OF THE SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system is a web-based clinic management system designed to computerize the
daily operations of the clinic. Its main purpose is to provide a single,
organized platform where patients, doctors, and front-desk/admin staff can
manage medical consultations efficiently.

Specifically, the system aims to:
  1. Simplify appointment booking and management for patients and staff.
  2. Reduce paper-based record keeping by storing patient records, vitals,
     laboratory results, and immunization records in one database.
  3. Organize patient flow at the clinic, including both scheduled
     appointments and walk-in (counter) patients.
  4. Support the clinic's maternal and child care services (consultations,
     prenatal monitoring, laboratory testing, and vaccination).
  5. Provide reports so the clinic can monitor performance, patient load,
     appointment status, and revenue.

The system is accessed through three (3) portals:
  - Patient Portal     – for patients/clients
  - Doctor Portal      – for the clinic's doctors
  - Admin Portal       – for front-desk/admin staff


II. USER ROLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PATIENT / CLIENT
   - Registers an account and books appointments online.
   - Views appointment status and history.
   - Requests laboratory tests.
   - Views vitals, lab results, and vaccination records.
   - Can reschedule or cancel appointments.
   - Can be registered as a walk-in by the frontline staff when arriving at the
     clinic without an appointment.

2. DOCTOR
   - Logs in to view and manage consultations.
   - Views appointments and updates appointment status.
   - Manages personal clinic schedule and blocks unavailable time.
   - Processes laboratory requests and encodes results.
   - Views patient laboratory and appointment information.

3. ADMIN / FRONT-DESK STAFF
   - Manages the whole system through the admin panel.
   - Books appointments on behalf of patients (counter booking).
   - Registers walk-in clients and manages the daily queue.
   - Manages patient records and doctor accounts.
   - Manages doctor schedules and time blocks.
   - Records laboratory tests, vitals, and vaccinations.
   - Views analytics, generates reports, and prints documents (appointment
     slips, lab results, vaccine cards).


III. SYSTEM FUNCTIONALITY (BY MODULE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. ACCOUNT / SECURITY MODULE
   - Patient registration with OTP (One-Time PIN) verification and secure
     password hashing.
   - Patient login, doctor login, and admin login.
   - Forgotten password recovery using OTP verification via email.
   - Admin password change with current-password verification.
   - Login lockout: the admin account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed
     login attempts to prevent brute-force attacks.
   - First-login password change: the default admin password (admin123) must be
     changed upon first login for security.
   - Session-based authentication so only logged-in users can access pages.

B. PATIENT PORTAL
   1. Home / Landing Page
      - Displays the clinic's services, doctors, and information.
      - Provides access to patient login and registration.
   2. Patient Dashboard
      - Shows the patient's appointments (sorted by status and date).
      - Shows requested laboratory tests with their current status and results.
      - Shows recorded vitals and vaccination history.
      - Lists the clinic's doctors with their specialties and schedules.
   3. Appointment Booking
      - Patient selects a doctor and the system shows only available dates and
        time slots based on the doctor's schedule.
      - Unavailable dates and time blocks are automatically excluded.
      - An appointment confirmation is generated and a confirmation slip/PDF
        can be printed and an email notice can be sent.
   4. My Appointments
      - Patient can view, reschedule, or cancel appointments.
      - Rescheduling updates the appointment history record.
   5. Laboratory Request
      - Patient can request laboratory tests (blood, urine/stool, infectious
        disease, pregnancy/hormonal, imaging, and other tests).
   6. Records Viewing
      - Patient can view their vitals, laboratory results, and vaccination
        records.

C. DOCTOR PORTAL
   1. Doctor Dashboard
      - Shows the doctor's appointments and assigned patients.
   2. Appointment Management
      - Doctor updates appointment status (pending, confirmed, completed,
        cancelled).
   3. Schedule Management
      - Doctor manages personal working schedule (days and time).
      - Doctor can add "time blocks" (unavailable / time-off) that are applied
        to the appointment system so patients cannot book those slots.
      - The doctor's schedule text is automatically synced and displayed to
        patients.
   4. Laboratory Management
      - Doctor requests laboratory tests for patients.
      - Doctor encodes and updates laboratory test results and status
        (pending, processing, completed).
   5. Printing
      - Doctor can print laboratory result PDFs.

D. ADMIN PANEL
   1. Dashboard
      - Shows summary statistics: total patients, total doctors, pending,
        confirmed, today's appointments, total appointments, and walk-ins
        today.
      - Quick actions: New Appointment (counter booking), Register Walk-In,
        View Patients, Record Vitals, New Lab Test, Manage Doctors, Schedules,
        View Walk-ins, and View Reports.
      - Recent Appointments table with status and payment updates.
      - Today's Walk-ins panel showing the current queue.
   2. Appointment Management
      - Front-desk books appointments for patients at the counter.
      - Manages appointment status and payment status (paid/unpaid).
      - Prints appointment slips.
      - Handles rescheduling and tracks reschedule history.
      - Searches and filters appointments by patient name, address, age,
        status, and priority.
   3. Patient Management
      - Lists all patients with contact details, patient type, age, weight,
        height, number of appointments, and last visit date.
      - Search filters by name, address, and age.
      - Registers walk-in clients (creates patient record + queue entry).
   4. Walk-in Queue Management  (recent update – see Section IV)
      - Dedicated "Walk-ins" page showing the daily queue.
   5. Doctor Management
      - Creates and manages doctor accounts with generated credentials.
      - Updates doctor profiles (name, specialty, schedule, experience).
   6. Schedule Management
      - Admin manages doctor schedules and time blocks for all doctors.
      - Time blocks are synced so the online booking calendar is accurate.
   7. Laboratory Management
      - Admin records and tracks laboratory tests for patients.
      - Updates test status and results.
      - Prints laboratory result PDFs.
   8. Vitals & Vaccines
      - Admin records patient vitals (weight, blood pressure, heart rate,
        fundal height, notes) per visit.
      - Records vaccinations with vaccine name, dose, administered date, and
        next due date.
      - Shows patient summary (latest weight, blood pressure, and next due
        vaccine).
      - Prints an immunization / vaccine card (PDF).
   9. Reports & Analytics
      - Generates reports with selectable period (today, week, month, year, or
        custom date range).
      - Filters by doctor, appointment status, and payment status.
      - Shows charts (appointments, patients, completed, cancelled, paid, and
        estimated revenue) using Chart.js.
      - Provides summary breakdowns and statistics for decision making.
   10. Archive / History
       - Stores and displays past/archived appointments and laboratory tests
         for record keeping.
   11. Walk-ins
       - Dedicated module for walk-in counter patients (see below).


IV. SCOPE: WALK-IN QUEUE MANAGEMENT  (RECENT UPDATE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This update introduces a queue-based system for registering and managing
walk-in (counter / walk-in without appointment) patients. The scope covers the
following:

   1. Walk-in Registration
      - Front-desk staff can register a walk-in patient from the Dashboard or
        the Patients page through a dedicated modal.
      - The form captures patient details (full name, contact number, address,
        patient type, age, weight, height) together with the selected DOCTOR
        and ARRIVAL TIME, which are required so the patient is placed in the
        correct queue. Email is optional (auto-generated if left blank), and a
        system-generated patient account is created.

   2. Dynamic Queue Numbering
      - Each walk-in is assigned a queue number that is generated per doctor,
        per day (e.g., Dr. A's queue #1, #2, #3; Dr. B's own #1, #2, ...).
      - The queue is ordered by arrival time; if two patients arrive at the
        same time, the earlier registration timestamp (and record ID) is used,
        ensuring first-come, first-served fairness.

   3. Walk-in Monitoring Page
      - A dedicated "Walk-ins" page (linked in the sidebar of every admin
        page) lists all walk-ins for a selected date (defaults to today).
      - Records are grouped by doctor and show the queue number, patient name,
        contact, patient type, arrival time, and current status.
      - A date filter lets staff review past days or switch back to today.

   4. Status Management
      - Staff can update each walk-in's status through the queue:
        Waiting -> In Service -> Served, or mark as Cancelled.
      - Summary counters are displayed for the selected day: Total, Waiting,
        In Service, Served, and Cancelled.

   5. Dashboard Visibility
      - The admin dashboard shows a "Walk-ins Today" statistic and a
        "Today's Walk-ins" panel so reception can monitor the clinic's current
        patient load at a glance.

   6. Data Persistence
      - A dedicated "walk_ins" database table stores the patient-doctor
        relationship, arrival date and time, status, and creation timestamp.
      - Indexed for fast lookup by doctor and date.

   OUT OF SCOPE (for the walk-in update):
      - Walk-ins do not consume appointment time slots and do not affect the
        doctor's schedule.
      - Walk-ins do not automatically create login credentials that patients
        must use (the patient account is created for record keeping).
      - The system does not automatically bill or print receipts for walk-in
        patients.


V. SUPPORTING FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Automated appointment reminders: a background script (cron / task
     scheduler) emails patients about upcoming appointments about 1 hour
     before the schedule.
   - Email notifications: appointment confirmation and reminder emails are
     sent through the system's mailer.
   - Printable documents: appointment confirmation/PDF, appointment slip,
     laboratory result PDF, and vaccine/immunization card PDF.
   - Responsive design and dark-mode support across the admin and patient
     interfaces.
   - Secure password storage using bcrypt password hashing.
